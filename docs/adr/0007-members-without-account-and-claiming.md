# Members without an account and claiming via a shared link

Expenses, shares and balances refer to a group member, not to a user. A member can exist without an account: any member adds them and enters expenses on their behalf. A person who opens the group link in the app can claim an existing member without an account (together with their whole history). The others get a notification and anyone can undo the claim. We accept the risk of impersonation, because among friends it is negligible, and one link for the group chat is key for convenience. Personal claim links are an additional option.
